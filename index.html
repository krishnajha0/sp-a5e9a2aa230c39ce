#WordMeaning

Practice like the real bee

  1. Pick your words: all of them, just the circled ones, or a range like 101 to 200.
  2. Hear the word and say it out loud before you spell, just like at the bee.
  3. Ask for clues: definition, part of speech and a sentence, like asking the pronouncer.
  4. Spell it out loud: tap the mic and say each letter slowly. Check the letters, then press Check spelling. Typing works too.
  5. Drill your misses until you get them all right.
